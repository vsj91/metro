Why the “nearest” station is not always the best station

The shortest straight-line distance may not produce the quickest trip. Station entrances, road crossings, BMTC availability, traffic, walking comfort and your exact building or gate can make another nearby station more practical. BLRGo is designed to help you plan the complete public-transport journey rather than only show a station name.
