About Namma Metro Navigator

Namma Metro Navigator is an independent route-planning tool for Bangalore Metro users. It helps riders search stations, estimate routes, view approximate fares, understand line changes, and see journey timing estimates.

Important Note

This website is not an official BMRCL website. Timetables, fares, platform directions, and arrival estimates are provided for general journey planning and may not always match live operating conditions.
