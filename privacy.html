Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Namma Metro and BMTC helps users estimate Bangalore Metro routes and BMTC bus options, fares, station stops, platform directions, and journey timing. This page explains how basic information may be collected when you use the site.

Location Data

The site may ask for browser location permission to estimate the nearest metro station. Location access only works if you allow it in your browser. Location is used in the page to calculate nearby stations and is not sold by this website.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand site usage, such as page visits, device/browser information, and general traffic patterns. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's partner sites policy page.

Third-Party Links

The site may link to official transport or external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the Contact page.
