Bangalore Metro and BMTC Bus Route Finder

Use this Bangalore Metro and BMTC bus route finder to plan Bengaluru transit trips across the Purple Line, Green Line, Yellow Line, and common BMTC bus corridors. The planner helps compare boarding and destination points, route stops, interchange or transfer points, estimated fare, platform direction, BMRCL timetable frequency, BMTC bus options, and live nearest metro station from GPS.

Search Places and Find the Nearest Metro Station

You can search for a Bengaluru station, area, address, or landmark and the planner will map it to the nearest available Namma Metro station. For example, searching Forum Mall Koramangala can select BTM Layout metro station, while searching UB City can select Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Station, Vidhana Soudha. This helps with searches such as nearest metro station to Forum Mall, nearest metro station to UB City, Bangalore Metro route from a landmark, and Bengaluru metro station near me.
